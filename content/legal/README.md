# Legal pages

`terms.md` and `privacy.md` here are served at `/terms` and `/privacy`. The text must come from
counsel (discovery §15, §16 item 22); none is shipped. Until both files exist the pages say the
documents are being finalised and the deployed preflight reports `legal.pages` as a warning.

Format: plain text. A line starting with `# ` is a heading; blank lines separate paragraphs.
