# Privacy Policy

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we") operates the private-label supplement platform. This policy explains what personal data we process and why.

# 1. Two roles

For Brand accounts (the businesses using the Platform) we are the controller of account data. For the customers of a Brand's store, we act as a processor on the Brand's behalf: we receive their order data from the Brand's store only to produce and ship their orders.

# 2. What we collect

Account data: name, email address, password (stored by our authentication provider, never by us in readable form), sign-in via Google if you choose it, organization and brand names, team membership and roles. Usage and security data: sign-in events, IP address and browser type, audit records of actions taken. Brand content: logos and label text you upload. Store customer data: order number, items, shipping address, and contact details included in the order.

# 3. Why we use it

To run your account, produce and ship orders, bill you, keep the Platform secure, send transactional emails (order received, action needed, shipped, label decisions, invitations), and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use store customers' data for our own marketing.

# 4. Who we share it with

Service providers that run the Platform for us: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email), Shopify (your connected store), and the manufacturer that fulfils orders (receives shipping details and order contents). Each processes data only on our instructions. We disclose data when the law requires it.

# 5. How long we keep it

Account data while your account is open and as required for accounting afterwards. Copies of dispatch spreadsheets containing customer addresses are deleted 90 days after the batch is completed. When a store customer asks the Brand's store to erase their data, we redact their name, contact details and address from our records (country is kept for tax and shipping records), except for orders still being shipped, which are redacted once delivered.

# 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to or restrict processing. Brand users can contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL]. Store customers should contact the Brand they bought from; we help the Brand respond (data access requests are answered with an export within 30 days).

# 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; store access tokens are encrypted at rest; each Brand's data is isolated by database access rules; files are private and shared only through short-lived links.
